Before you begin

Charms are equipped and removed while sitting at a Bench. Each charm requires a number of Charm Notches. The Knight begins with three Notches and can obtain eight additional Notches.

Transformations: Fragile Heart, Fragile Greed and Fragile Strength can be upgraded into unbreakable versions. Kingsoul becomes Void Heart, while Grimmchild can be replaced by Carefree Melody through a different Grimm Troupe quest outcome. These alternate forms have separate entries in this compendium.

The map sheet immediately after each entry is intentionally blank. Fill its image source with your own location map. On screen, each charm is followed by a separate full-screen map sheet; in print, the sheets are formatted as A4 portrait pages.
